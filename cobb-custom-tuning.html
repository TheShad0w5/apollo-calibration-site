SUBARU & OTHER SUPPORTED PLATFORMS

Choose the tune
for the car you actually have.

Looking for a Subaru WRX e-tune, STI custom tune or help with your COBB Accessport? Share the exact model year, engine, transmission, market and device part number. Your modifications and fuel help determine the right approach.

Forester XT, Legacy GT/XT, Outback XT, Ascent and BRZ owners can also inquire about their tuning options. Availability depends on the exact vehicle and tuning platform. Crosstrek and base Impreza are not advertised as COBB-supported applications.

Owners of other COBB-supported vehicles are welcome. Keith confirms vehicle compatibility and the available custom-tuning service before accepting work. A manufacturer's name alone does not establish support.

What we can work on.

Custom calibration

Discuss fuel, ignition, boost and drivability refinement for your supported setup. The calibration plan starts with the hardware, fuel and intended use.

Remote e-tuning

Follow the agreed process for flashing and recording datalogs, then send requested data for review. Collect data only in a safe, legal environment.

Datalog review & revisions

Review vehicle behavior and determine the next step. Revision scope, follow-up support and any changes to hardware or fuel are agreed before work.

GETTING STARTED

From inquiry to calibration.

1. Confirm your setup

Send vehicle details, Accessport part number, modification list, fuel and current concerns. Keith confirms fit, required equipment, pricing and availability.

2. Agree on the process

Discuss whether remote e-tuning, diagnostics or a dyno session suits the project, and what information is needed before work begins.

3. Review & refine

Use the agreed logging and revision process to assess vehicle behavior and refine the calibration. Mechanical issues may need attention first.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can I buy any Accessport and get a custom tune?

No. Device compatibility, vehicle specifications and available tuning support must be confirmed first. Send your setup before purchasing equipment.

Do you offer a fixed number of revisions or a guaranteed power gain?

Scope and revisions are agreed for the project. Results depend on the hardware, fuel, mechanical condition and validation process; there is no universal power figure for every setup.

What if I change parts after tuning?

Contact Keith before changing hardware or fuel. The existing calibration may need revision, and further work is scoped separately.
