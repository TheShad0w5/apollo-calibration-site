YOUR QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can Keith help with Emtron startup or sync-loss problems?

Yes. Initial review can cover configuration, trigger signals and timing validation. Some checks require hands-on electrical or mechanical work; Keith will identify what can be reviewed remotely and what needs local testing.

Is a mechanical or wiring problem something a tune can fix?

A calibration cannot repair a mechanical fault or incorrect wiring. Review helps identify the next checks; some issues must be resolved locally before tuning continues.
