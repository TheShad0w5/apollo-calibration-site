THE APPROACH

A tune starts with
understanding the vehicle.

A reputation in tuning is built through years of working with real engines, real problems and the people behind each build.

Keith’s background spans ECU calibration, dyno development, drivability refinement and complex troubleshooting. That experience informs how he approaches both factory ECU tuning and standalone engine-management systems.

A vehicle’s hardware, available fuel, mechanical condition and intended use all matter. The first conversation is about your setup and what you want it to do; the next step is a tuning or diagnostic plan that fits those details.

Whether you are bringing your first modified street car or a developed motorsport project, you can ask questions in plain language and discuss the work directly with Keith.

Where that experience applies.

COBB custom tuning

Custom Accessport tuning and remote e-tuning for supported factory ECU platforms, with a strong Subaru focus.

Standalone engine management

Emtron, Haltech, Link ECU and MoTeC support spanning setup, calibration and system diagnosis.

Technical understanding

Explore the calibration knowledge base for articles on datalogs, boost control, sensors and engine-management fundamentals.
