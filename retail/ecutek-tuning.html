Toyota GR tuning

COBB or EcuTek?

Some vehicles, such as the R35 GT-R, have more than one tuning route. An Accessport license or map is not an EcuTek license or map. Tell me which equipment and calibration you already have before buying anything.

My COBB guide lists Mazda, Ford, Nissan, Toyota, Volkswagen, Audi, Honda and other supported applications by model. Older Skyline R32/R33/R34 projects are discussed separately around the installed ECU; see the Haltech R32 archive.

Plan the complete package.

Send your year, model, trim, engine, transmission, market, modifications, fuel and current tuning-device details. I’ll confirm equipment, licensing, any unlock requirements, available features, pricing and revision scope in your quote.

Remote tuning, dyno sessions and worldwide travel are arranged around the project. RaceROM features, flex fuel and transmission tuning are individually assessed and are not automatically included.
