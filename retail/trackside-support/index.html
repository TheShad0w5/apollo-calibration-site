Support between sessions.

Trackside work can include reviewing datalogs, evaluating boost and torque delivery, investigating sensor behavior, and assessing fuel or ignition strategy. The aim is to understand each run before deciding what to change for the next one.

Calibration adjustments depend on your ECU, installed sensors, mechanical condition and the event’s requirements. ECU data is considered alongside driver feedback and observations from the crew.

Plan the event together.

Send the event location and dates, vehicle and engine details, ECU platform, fuel, current calibration and testing goals. Include known issues and the support available from your team.

On-site availability, travel, scope, costs and access to the vehicle are agreed in advance. For preparation before the event, explore remote calibration review or dyno tuning.

Start with your setup.

Tell Keith what you drive, which ECU you use, what has changed and what you want to improve. Scope, pricing and availability are agreed before work begins.
