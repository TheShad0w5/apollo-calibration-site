Before you order.

Is a custom e-tune the same as an OTS map?

A custom e-tune is developed through the agreed logging and revision process for your vehicle. An OTS map is intended for a specified combination. I offer custom tuning based on your individual setup.

How do the eight revisions work?

Most $400 custom e-tunes include eight revisions. We use them to evaluate the requested logs, adjust the calibration and confirm the result. They are an allowance, not eight mandatory rounds: a mechanically sound, consistent setup may need fewer. Mechanical faults, changing parts or fuel, and incomplete logs can require additional work. I discuss any added scope before proceeding.

How quickly will I receive a file or revision?

Same-day delivery is often possible. Allow up to one business day after the required information, usable logs and any applicable payment are received. Business hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Pacific. This applies to each ready-to-review step; completing the whole tune depends on your car, logging and feedback.

Can I buy a base map first?

The $50 base map service supplies a starting calibration for approved use and an agreed combination. It is not a completed custom tune; follow-up work is scoped separately.
