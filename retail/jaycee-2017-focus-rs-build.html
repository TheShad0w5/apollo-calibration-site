Dyno & development

THE BUILD / ENGINE, TURBO & CALIBRATION

A complete project, developed over time.

AWD’s March 2023 update shows the car in the workshop and describes the plan for a Speed Perf6rmanc3 built long block and a turbo upgrade after an engine failure. The later archive includes a June 2024 configuration labelled “PTE 93 oct” and a September configuration labelled “w-meth.”

The work developed as a complete build, rather than a single map change on an otherwise unchanged car. The dated results below document those stages; they do not isolate the gain from one component, fuel change or tune.

Planning a similar RS? Send the engine and turbo specification, fuel-system details, tuning device and intended fuel. For a setup using injection hardware, include its controller, fluid mixture, logging and failsafe arrangements so I can review the full combination.
