THE PROJECT

A turbo upgrade needs a complete calibration.

Juan’s STI brings together a Garrett G30-900 and Haltech engine management. My role is the calibration: developing the tune around the car’s hardware and the way its systems work together.

For a similar project, we start with your engine, fuel system, turbo, ECU and sensors. That information determines the setup checks and tuning scope before a session is arranged.

THE FINISHED CAR

Black sedan. Bronze wheels. Juan’s STI.

DYNO ARCHIVE

Juan’s recorded results.

The archived Mustang dyno report for Juan’s 2013 STI records 737 hp and 569 lb-ft in its weather-corrected channels.

Session details

The report was printed August 13, 2026. Its acquisition field reads March 10 without a year. Fuel, boost and turbo configuration are not listed, so this record is not presented as a verified G30-900 result or as the setup shown in the August 2025 footage.

Planning your own Haltech STI?

Send your current hardware, fuel, ECU model and goals. I’ll help establish the calibration scope and whether remote support, a coordinated road session or a customer-arranged dyno session fits your project.
