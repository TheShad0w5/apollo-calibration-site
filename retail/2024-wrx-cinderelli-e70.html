Power is one part of the package.

AWD Tuning’s original post documents Mary Fields’ 2024 Subaru WRX, Cinderelli, at 388 whp and 417 wtq on E70. The same feature records an XClutch 240 mm single-plate clutch installation.

Documented setup
  • Vehicle: 2024 Subaru WRX
  • Fuel reported: E70
  • Output reported: 388 whp / 417 wtq
  • Clutch: XClutch 240 mm single-plate

Development in the dyno archive

My archived Mustang Dynamometer report records an earlier Cinderelli test on April 15, 2025, with the setup comment “Cobb package FMIC.” The individual run report lists 371.5 hp at 5,336 rpm and 411.8 lb-ft at 3,586 rpm. FMIC refers to a front-mount intercooler.

This is a separate test from the 388 whp / 417 wtq E70 feature above. It documents another stage of the car’s development; the figures are not a before-and-after comparison on identical hardware and fuel.

Why this example matters

A tuning discussion should include how the car will be used and which supporting parts are installed. This example puts the clutch alongside the engine’s reported output, making the drivetrain part of the conversation.

What the numbers tell us

These are the figures reported for this particular car on E70. They are not a promised outcome for another WRX or another fuel blend. The source does not provide a complete modification list or enough dyno conditions to make a like-for-like comparison.
