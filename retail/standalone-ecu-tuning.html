What stage is your build at?

Planning or first start

Share the wiring plan, engine specification and equipment list. We define the setup work and requirements before a calibration session.

Running, but needs refinement

Describe the behavior, previous work and available logs. Starting, idle, transient response and engine output all form part of the assessment.

Ready for the dyno

Send your facility and location details. We agree on the checks, session scope and whether remote support or an in-person visit suits the project.

Prepare your setup information.

  • Engine, displacement, camshafts, induction and intended use.
  • Exact ECU, firmware, current calibration and wiring documentation.
  • Throttle arrangement, injectors, fuel delivery, fuel type and logged sensors.
  • Running condition, known faults, location and available dyno facilities.

Standalone work, equipment and travel are individually quoted. The standard Subaru e-tune price does not automatically cover a standalone project.

Planning questions.

Can you work with my existing ECU?

Send the exact model, software and configuration. I confirm access, required measurements and scope before agreeing to the work.

Can this be handled remotely?

Some work can be supported remotely with suitable equipment and someone competent at the vehicle. Other projects need an in-person session. We decide after reviewing the installation and current condition.

Do you work with shops and engine builders?

Yes. I offer project support and worldwide dyno visits for owners and shops. Responsibilities, facilities and scheduling are agreed for each job.
