THOMAS / 2004 SUBARU STI / HALTECH

Built in the workshop.
Developed on the dyno.

Thomas’s blue 2004 STI brings together engine-build work, Haltech calibration and dated Mustang dyno records. The later individual report records 767.3 hp and 562.2 lb-ft with WCF correction for the EFR 9280 / 34 psi configuration.

Development historyWorkshop photosOriginal dyno records

THE DEVELOPMENT RECORD

Different stages, clearly recorded.

February–March 2024 · Workshop development

The named AWD photo set records the blue STI, engine work and assembly in the workshop. The accompanying February clip shows an earlier engine-bay configuration before the later development documented here.

December 27, 2024 · Haltech / E80 record

The individual Mustang report records 679.5 hp and 534.9 lb-ft with WCF correction. The source file identifies the HT/E80 configuration; the report’s test-run time is December 27 at 1:44 p.m.

July 8, 2025 · EFR 9280 / 34 psi record

The later individual report records 767.3 hp and 562.2 lb-ft with WCF correction. Its uncorrected peaks are 726.9 hp and 532.6 lb-ft. The file identifies the EFR 9280 and 34 psi setup; fuel is not specified for this session.

May 2026 · Later workshop archive

A separate named photo set documents the car and underbody hardware in the workshop. These later photographs are not presented as a new dyno result or as proof of the exact July 2025 configuration.
