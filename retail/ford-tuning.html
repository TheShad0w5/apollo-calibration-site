START WITH THE EXACT MODEL

Which Ford are you building?

Focus ST & Focus RS

The ST and RS use different applications; an Accessport for one is not automatically correct for the other. Send the model year, market, device part number and current map, along with intake, intercooler, turbo and fueling changes.

Tell me what you want from the car: daily response, track use or refinement after hardware changes. We agree on the fuel and tuning scope before starting.

COBB Focus RS device reference ↗

Fiesta ST

Confirm that the car is an ST and identify its model year and market. Include the intake, intercooler, turbo, boost-control and fueling parts actually fitted; a parts list is more useful than a stage label.

A calibration needs to match the fuel you can consistently buy. State the pump-fuel grade and rating system, plus any ethanol blend and how you measure it.

COBB Focus ST / Fiesta ST device reference ↗

Raptor

Start with the full model: F-150 Raptor, Raptor R, Bronco Raptor or Ranger Raptor. Include the year, engine, transmission and market. The Raptor name alone does not identify the correct tuning product or confirm custom-tuning access.

Describe towing, terrain, tire changes and how you use the truck. Engine and transmission calibration are reviewed separately; an engine-tuning device does not establish transmission support.

PREPARE THE CAR AND THE INFORMATION

What to send before ordering.

Your vehicle and device

Year, exact model, engine, transmission and country/market. Add the Accessport part number, installation status, current calibration and any previous tuner information.

Hardware, fuel and condition

List exact parts and the fuel you will use. Describe warning lights, misfires, leaks or other concerns before tuning; calibration cannot repair a mechanical fault.

Your goal and location

Explain what you want to improve and whether you prefer remote tuning or an in-person session. For dyno work, include your location and available facility.

THE TUNING PROCESS

Confirm. Log. Refine.

  1. Review the setup. I confirm vehicle support, equipment, fuel, pricing and the work included before payment.
  2. Prepare and log. Follow the instructions for your file and record the channels requested for your exact Ford. Subaru parameter lists are not a Ford logging checklist.
  3. Review and revise. Send the original logs and driving feedback through the agreed delivery channel. I use them to develop the calibration and confirm when the tune is complete.

Use a suitable battery-support supply for flashing and follow the vehicle-specific procedure. A base map or intermediate revision has its own operating instructions; do not assume it is ready for full-load use.

Before you buy.

Can I use a second-hand Accessport?

Confirm the part number, supported vehicle and that it was properly uninstalled from the previous car. Send those details before buying; a device being physically able to plug in does not establish fitment or licensing.

Can I tune for an ethanol blend?

Send the intended blend, fuel-system hardware and how ethanol content is measured. I review the equipment and available software before agreeing on a fixed-blend or flex-fuel strategy. Switching fuels is not automatically supported by every map.

How much power will my Ford make?

That depends on the engine, turbo, fuel, hardware, condition and test method. We set goals for the complete setup and evaluate drivability as well as output. Another car’s dyno figure is not a promised result.

Can we use a dyno instead of remote logs?

Yes, by arrangement. Send your location and facility details so we can plan instrumentation, scheduling and the work needed. Dyno access and travel are agreed separately.
