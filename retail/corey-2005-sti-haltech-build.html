CALIBRATION BY KEITH FIELDS

The hardware and the tune, together.

Corey Jones’s white 2005 STI pairs a built engine with a Precision 6466 and standalone Haltech management. I calibrated this combination on E80. AWD’s June 2024 feature also identifies Radium Engineering and Fuel Injector Clinic fueling components, plus a rebuilt six-speed transmission.

For your own project, the complete combination matters: fuel delivery, sensors, boost control and mechanical condition all shape the calibration work. Share that information when you inquire so we can plan the session around your car.
