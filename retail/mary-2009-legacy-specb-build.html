EFR 9180 → Precision 6870

NOVEMBER 2015 · WANNAGOFAST TEXAS

173 mph in the half-mile.

The earlier combination paired a sleeved AWD Tuning Stage 4 engine with a Full Race twin-scroll kit and BorgWarner EFR 9180. AWD’s November 16, 2015 video documents the 173-mph run and describes a COBB-tuned E85 setup.

Open the original AWD video ↗

The period build list includes JE pistons, Pauter X-beam rods, a stock STI crank, GSC S3 cams, oversized valves and ported heads. It also lists a stock six-speed transmission, Driveshaft Shop rear axles and carbon-fiber driveshaft, and KW coilovers.

SEPTEMBER 2016 · QUARTER-MILE

Into the nines: 9.93 at 144.

By the September 13, 2016 video, the Legacy had moved to a Precision 6870, a revised engine combination and drag-focused suspension. AWD’s footage records the 9.93-second pass at 144 mph.

Open the original AWD video ↗

The accompanying build description reports 803 whp and 639 wtq at 40 psi on E80, using the stock ECU and COBB Accessport. Those figures describe this later configuration; the original November 2015 dyno peaks are listed separately below.

THE 2016 COMBINATION

More than an engine and turbo.

The nine-second setup brought together the engine, fuel supply, cooling, clutch, axles and suspension. These specifications come from AWD’s original description of the 2016 pass.

Engine & cylinder heads

  • AWD Tuning Stage 4 engine with Darton sleeves
  • Manley pistons, rods and crankshaft
  • 14 mm head studs
  • Kelford 282 cams and Ferrea valvetrain
  • Heads ported by Headgames Motorworks

Turbo & airflow

  • Precision 6870 turbo
  • Full Race twin-scroll kit
  • Process West intake manifold
  • Custom Dynosaur Performance front-mount intercooler
  • PERRIN exhaust

Fuel, oil & monitoring

  • Fuel Injector Clinic 2150 cc injectors
  • IAG fuel rails and TGV deletes
  • Radium dual in-tank hanger with twin Walbro 450 pumps
  • Custom fuel cooler and oil cooler
  • Killer B oil pan and pickup; ATI damper
  • IAG air/oil separator and Zeitronix gauges

Drivetrain & suspension

  • Competition Clutch triple-disc clutch
  • Driveshaft Shop rear axles and driveshaft
  • Kartboy pitch stop
  • Feal custom drag coilovers
  • Stock ECU calibrated with COBB Accessport

NOVEMBER 2015 · ORIGINAL MUSTANG ACTIVEREPORT

752.6 hp. 600.8 lb-ft.

The original PowerDyne ActiveReport for Mary’s 2009 Legacy SpecB records weather-corrected maximum power of 752.6 hp at 6,840 rpm and maximum torque of 600.8 lb-ft at 5,765 rpm. These are the report’s peak fields, not values estimated from a smoothed graph.

AWD’s 2015 half-mile video rounds the setup to 753 whp / 600 wtq on E85. Its description lists 39 psi; the dyno archive filename labels the session 38 psi. The later 803-whp E80 figure belongs to the 2016 configuration above.
