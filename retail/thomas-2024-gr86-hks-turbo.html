FEBRUARY 7, 2025 / MUSTANG DYNAMOMETER

337 hp. 271 lb-ft. One documented setup.

The report printed February 7, 2025 lists run 3 at 12:17 p.m., with peaks of 337 hp and 271 lb-ft. The other two runs list 309 and 307 hp. These are separate recorded pulls, not a stock baseline and not a measured gain from one modification.

The file identifies the HKS turbo, 10 psi and 93-octane setup. Those details make the record useful alongside the photos, while keeping the result specific to this car and session.
