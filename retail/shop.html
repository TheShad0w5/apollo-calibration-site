DELIVERY & REVISIONS

Usually same day.
Within one business day.

Initial map delivery and each agreed tuning revision are usually completed the same business day and will not exceed one business day once everything needed is received.

  • Initial delivery: the clock starts once I have approved your setup, payment is confirmed and all required vehicle details and files have been received.
  • Revisions: the clock starts when I receive the complete requested datalogs and feedback. Send them in the same email thread with your inquiry reference.
  • Business hours: Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Pacific. Requests received outside these hours begin the next business day; weekends are not business days.

This turnaround applies to each file delivery or revision, not completion of the entire tuning process. Time spent collecting logs, supplying missing information or resolving vehicle issues is separate. I’ll explain what is needed if work cannot proceed.

Most $400 custom e-tunes include 8 revisions. Hardware or fuel changes, diagnostic work and additional revisions may require a revised scope agreed before work continues.

YOUR TUNE, STEP BY STEP

Review first. Then tune.

  1. Send your setup. Include exact parts, fuel, transmission and current tune. Keep your inquiry reference.
  2. I confirm the plan. Compatibility, price, included revisions and expected timing are agreed before payment.
  3. Pay your QuickBooks invoice. Your invoice identifies your approved setup and inquiry reference.
  4. Receive your instructions. Once payment is confirmed, I explain the next steps and how to submit datalogs.
  5. Work through revisions. Keep replies in the same email thread. I track files and revisions against your inquiry reference.

Most custom e-tunes are $400 with 8 revisions. Hardware or fuel changes and diagnostic work may need a revised scope. File downloads do not count as tuning revisions.
