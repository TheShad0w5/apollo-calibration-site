THE BUILD / IAG SHORTBLOCK & KELFORD CAMS

Plan the whole combination.

Chris’s build pairs an IAG shortblock with Kelford cams and extensive supporting upgrades. A built engine changes the conversation: the calibration still needs to match the fuel system, turbo, sensors and intended use.

If you are planning a similar WRX, send the exact shortblock, camshaft and turbo specifications along with your fuel-system details and tuning device. “Built motor” alone does not establish the right calibration or a safe power target.

These records do not establish when each component was installed, so the dated sessions below are not presented as a before-and-after test of the shortblock or cams.
