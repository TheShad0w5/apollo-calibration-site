The engine behind the result

AWD’s original Facebook post names Armando and his 2015 WRX while documenting work on the FA20 cylinder heads. The shop’s August 30, 2018 YouTube feature identifies an in-house Stage 4 closed-deck FA20 block and specifically credits Keith Fields’ tuning, alongside the AWD team’s build work.

The video, titled 2015 WRX FA20 6262, reports 591 awhp. Its description identifies the stock camshafts and valve springs as restrictions at that stage. That detail gives the power figure context: the engine package and valvetrain were part of the development story, rather than the result being presented as a tune alone.

The MoTeC chapter

I confirm that this is the same white WRX I identify with a 666-whp result on MoTeC engine management. Together, the documented FA20 engine work and my standalone calibration account make this a useful example of experience extending beyond factory-ECU tuning.

The 591-awhp figure belongs to the August 2018 video. The 666-whp MoTeC figure comes from my firsthand account; the date, fuel, boost pressure, ECU model and final hardware combination for that configuration have not been specified. Specifications from the earlier video are not assumed to describe the MoTeC setup.
