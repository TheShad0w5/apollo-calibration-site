START WITH THE RIGHT INFORMATION

What to send Keith.

Your exact Link ECU model, PCLink file, engine and trigger details, injector data and available logs. Trigger scope captures and wiring notes can help when investigating startup or sync issues.

Scope, pricing, availability and the required physical checks are confirmed before work begins. Individual owners and professional shops are welcome.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Do you review Link G4X and PCLink files remotely?

Remote file and datalog review is available by agreement. Send the ECU model and project description first so Keith can confirm what files and physical checks are needed.

Is a mechanical or wiring problem something a tune can fix?

A calibration cannot repair a mechanical fault or incorrect wiring. Review helps identify the next checks; some issues must be resolved locally before tuning continues.
