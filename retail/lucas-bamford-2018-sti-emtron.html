THE COMPLETE CAR

More than a peak-power number.

Keith singles out Lucas’s STI as his “ultimate GT STI”. That is his personal assessment of the overall build: a car that belongs in his history alongside the competition cars, while representing a different goal for the finished package.

The dyno archive identifies the vehicle as a 2018 Subaru STI and Lucas Bamford as the owner. It preserves multiple stages of the car’s development, including an earlier EFR 7163 configuration and later reports labeled Emtron flex fuel.

Documented build details

  • Vehicle: 2018 Subaru STI
  • Owner: Lucas Bamford
  • Tuning: Keith Fields, during his AWD Tuning work
  • Engine: AWD 770 / Rallispec long-block
  • Camshafts: Kelford 264s
  • 2025 turbo: ETS rotated kit with BorgWarner EFR 7670
  • Fuel system: AWD fuel system
  • Driver control: three SI-Drive power modes
  • Later ECU platform: Emtron
  • Earlier turbo reference: EFR 7163, named in the 2022 archive filenames
  • 2025 fuel labels: 93 octane and E80
  • Dyno: Mustang Dynamometer

The 2025 hardware is documented in AWD’s January 15 Instagram feature, which Keith confirms shows Lucas’s car. The earlier EFR 7163 reference belongs to the development archive. Exact ECU model, boost pressure and complete internal specifications are not stated.

WATCH THE BUILD / JANUARY 15, 2025

560-plus at the wheels.
Three ways to drive it.

AWD’s video documents the Rallispec long-block, Kelford cams and rotated EFR 7670 combination. Its caption explains that the SI-Drive selector controls three distinct power experiences, with smooth drivability remaining a priority. That is the detail behind Keith’s GT description: the calibration considers how the driver uses the car, as well as what it produces on the dyno.

The tuning brief

Preserve a smooth driving experience while giving the owner distinct levels of performance through the factory selector.

The post reports more than 560 horsepower at the wheels. The dated E80 report supplies the more precise 564.5 hp / 550.2 lb-ft result. These sources document the same build chapter, while the original report remains the reference for its test date and maxima.

The owner’s road report.

On February 1, 2025, Lucas shared the car after AWD Tuning’s finishing work. He identified the new Rallispec engine and Emtron ECU, reported a trouble-free 700-mile drive home, and praised the smooth ride. It is a useful owner account of the finished car’s road manners alongside the dyno record.

THE DEVELOPMENT RECORD

A build with history.

2022 · EFR 7163 development

The archive contains EFR 7163 files naming both 93 octane and E70. One individual report records a July 10, 2022 run at 443.5 hp and 394.5 lb-ft. Because its filename names two fuels, that run is not assigned a specific blend here.

2022 runs · Reprinted in 2023

Other reports preserve a July 10 run at 418.6 hp / 381.1 lb-ft and a July 19 run at 548.5 hp / 460.5 lb-ft. Their filenames identify 93 octane and E85 respectively. Both were printed in May 2023, illustrating why export dates are not tuning dates.

2025 · Emtron chapter

The January reports document the Emtron stage on 93 octane and E80. Keith’s firsthand account connects the car to his standalone calibration work and his description of the finished STI as a grand-touring build.

How this history is documented

This feature uses Keith’s firsthand account and Lucas’s original reports from the AWD dyno archive. The reports identify Lucas by name and the car by year, make and model. Customer registration details are omitted from this page.
