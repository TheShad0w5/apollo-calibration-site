A tune developed around your setup.

  1. Review the car. Send the year, transmission, market, ECU/ROM ID, current tuning history, hardware and fuel.
  2. Agree on the service. We confirm compatibility, equipment, price and revision scope. Remote work uses requested logs and driving feedback; in-person sessions require coordination.
  3. Prepare and log. Follow the instructions supplied for your car. Mechanical readiness and usable data come before increasing load.
  4. Refine and confirm. I review the requested data and adjust the calibration through the agreed revisions, then confirm when the tune is complete.

Equipment and existing tunes.

Tell me your Atlas version, computer and adapter model before purchasing anything. We confirm the supported connection and logging equipment for your ECU and the work planned.

If your car currently uses an Accessport or another tuning platform, discuss the transition first. Accessport maps and hardware are not interchangeable with Atlas. A modified car may need a coordinated calibration change rather than being driven on a stock file.

Atlas tuning questions.

How much does an Atlas tune cost?

Request a quote for your vehicle and goals. I confirm the calibration work, revision allowance and any licensing or equipment costs before you order. The listed Accessport prices apply to Accessport services.

Can we discuss flex fuel or additional features?

Yes. Include the fuel-system hardware, ethanol sensor and controller details, and the features you want. I confirm what is suitable for your ECU, software and setup before including it in the scope.

Is Atlas still open source?

NAMR states that current Atlas software is no longer open source. Personal-use availability and commercial licensing are separate from the cost of a custom tuning service. Read the official Atlas FAQ ↗

Are the WRX build results on this site Atlas results?

Each build page identifies its own platform and development history. A WRX result from another platform is not presented as an Atlas result or a promise of output for your car.
