START WITH THE RIGHT INFORMATION

What to send Keith.

Your Haltech ECU model, software version, calibration file, engine and fuel-system details, and current datalogs if available. Describe whether the issue occurs during cranking, idle, warm-up or load.

Scope, pricing, availability and the required physical checks are confirmed before work begins. Individual owners and professional shops are welcome.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can I get help with Haltech setup before dyno tuning?

Yes. Setup review can help identify missing sensor information, timing checks or configuration issues before a tuning session. Remote support and dyno arrangements are agreed for your specific project.

Is a mechanical or wiring problem something a tune can fix?

A calibration cannot repair a mechanical fault or incorrect wiring. Review helps identify the next checks; some issues must be resolved locally before tuning continues.
