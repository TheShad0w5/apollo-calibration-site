YOUR QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can I buy any Accessport and get a custom tune?

No. Device compatibility, vehicle specifications and available tuning support must be confirmed first. Send your setup before purchasing equipment.

Do you offer a fixed number of revisions or a guaranteed power gain?

Most e-tunes are $400 with 8 revisions. I confirm the scope for your setup before payment. Results depend on the hardware, fuel, mechanical condition and validation process; there is no universal power figure for every setup.

What if I change parts after tuning?

Contact me before changing hardware or fuel. The existing calibration may need revision, and further work is scoped separately.
