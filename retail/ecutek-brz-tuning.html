Custom tuning and EcuTek packages.

I can help plan the tuning service and the EcuTek equipment or licensing your project needs. Tell me what you already own so the package matches your existing setup.

Remote tuning is available following a compatibility and logging review. Dyno tuning and worldwide travel sessions can also be arranged. Pricing, included revisions, hardware and licenses are confirmed in your individual quote before payment.

Factory ECU calibration

EcuTek reprograms the supported original ECU; it is a different route from installing a standalone engine-management system. Available functions depend on the ECU, ROM, software and package.

RaceROM and flex fuel

Where supported, we can discuss features such as map switching and flex-fuel calibration. Ethanol sensing, fuel-system capacity and the installed hardware must suit the intended strategy. Features are agreed individually rather than assumed to be included with every tune.

FEATURED BUILD / TOYOTA GR86

White 2024 GR86. HKS turbo. EcuTek tuning.

Thomas’s white 2024 Toyota GR86 is an example of my work bringing forced induction to the second-generation platform. I tuned its low-boost HKS turbo combination with EcuTek; the February 2025 dyno record documents a 10 psi setup on 93-octane fuel.

My focus is how the complete package drives: transient throttle response, the relationship between throttle demand and boost, and smooth, usable power. The calibration needs to suit the hardware, fuel and intended use—not just produce a peak number.

From the original dyno record

The Mustang Dynamometer report printed February 7, 2025 lists a peak of 337 hp and 271 lb-ft for run 3, recorded that day at 12:17 p.m. The other two runs in the report list 309 and 307 hp. These are the report’s recorded results for this car, not promised gains or a stock-versus-tuned comparison.

The source is my archived “Thomas 2024 GR86 HKS turbo 10 psi 93 oct” dyno report. Hardware, fuel and test conditions matter when comparing results; the goal is a complete, repeatable driving experience.

BRZ & GR86 EcuTek questions

Can you tune both the old and new BRZ?

Yes. I offer tuning for the first-generation FA20 and second-generation FA24 BRZ, subject to checking your exact ECU, model year, transmission and hardware combination.

Is EcuTek hardware or a license included?

Your quote will state exactly what is included. Send details of equipment and licensing you already have before purchasing anything.

Can you tune a turbocharged or supercharged BRZ?

Send the complete build specifications. I review the engine, forced-induction kit, fuel system and logging requirements before accepting the project and setting its scope.

How much power will it make?

That depends on the hardware, fuel, engine condition and test conditions. I discuss realistic goals after reviewing your car rather than promise a figure from a different build.
