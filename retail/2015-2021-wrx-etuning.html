BEFORE ORDERING

Start with the right information.

Vehicle and tuning tools

This page covers the FA20-powered VA WRX, not the EJ-powered STI. Send the model year, market, transmission, ECU information and Accessport details. Other tuning tools need an individual compatibility review before payment.

Your hardware and fuel

List the exact intake, MAF housing, turbo, intercooler, boost-control hardware and fuel-system modifications. Include engine work and any swaps. Changing hardware or fuel during tuning can change the agreed scope.

Logging and mechanical readiness

For 2015–2017 WRX remote fueling work, plan on an external wideband with a verified logging method. For 2018–2021 cars, I confirm the measurement plan individually. A gauge without usable recorded data is not a substitute for the requested log.

Tell me about any misfires, leaks, warning lights or other known faults before we start. I may ask for mechanical checks before tuning. Follow the logging instructions supplied for your car.

WHAT TO EXPECT

How your e-tune works.

  1. Confirm the setup. Choose the service for your car, fuel and tuning device. Ask me first if compatibility or the work needed is uncertain.
  2. Order and complete onboarding. Pay through secure QuickBooks checkout, then provide the vehicle, hardware and fuel details needed to prepare your calibration.
  3. Log, review and refine. Follow the instructions for each file and send the requested logs and driving feedback through your agreed delivery channel. I review the data and return revisions.
  4. Finish with the approved file. I confirm when the tuning process is complete. Follow the instructions supplied with each map, including any limits during development.

What the revision allowance covers

Most custom e-tunes include up to eight calibration revisions for the approved setup. Each revision follows a review of usable logs and feedback; downloading the same file again does not use a revision. Changing hardware or fuel, diagnosing faults or needing extra revisions can change the scope. I discuss any additional work and cost with you first.

Typical turnaround

Initial files and revisions are usually sent the same day, or within one business day once I have the approved setup, required information or logs, and any payment due. Business hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Pacific. The full tuning process depends on your logging, feedback and the car’s condition.

BEFORE YOU BUY / CHOOSE YOUR TUNE

Which tuning service fits my Subaru?

Should I choose a single-fuel or flex-fuel tune?

The listed Accessport services are $400 for single fuel and $600 for flex fuel. Tell me what fuel you can consistently obtain and the exact fuel-system and ethanol-sensing hardware installed. A flex-fuel selection does not establish that your ECU, software and hardware are compatible. I confirm the combination and scope before tuning. Hardware, licenses and diagnostic work may be separate. Compare the listed services →

Does my car qualify for a retune?

The listed Accessport retunes are for an existing Keith calibration: $200 single fuel or $300 flex fuel. Include your previous order, current file and every hardware or fuel change. If the car has another tuner's calibration, or you do not know its history, ask me to review it before selecting a retune. I confirm whether the change fits the retune service or needs a different scope.

I have a 2015–2021 STI. Is this the right page?

This page is for the FA20-powered WRX. An STI from the same model years belongs on the EJ WRX / STI service page. Include engine or ECU swaps in your inquiry rather than choosing by body shape or model year alone.

Should I buy a wideband before ordering my tune?

For 2015–2017 WRX remote fueling work, plan on an external wideband with a verified way to record its data. For 2018–2021 WRXs, I confirm the measurement plan individually. Ask about the exact controller, sensor and logging connection before buying; a gauge or harness alone does not establish a usable log. Review the wideband logging questions →

Can I tune now and install more parts during the revisions?

Send the planned upgrades before we start. The revision allowance develops the agreed setup; it is not an allowance for an unlimited sequence of new hardware or fuel combinations. Changes during tuning may need a revised scope. I discuss additional work before proceeding, so we can decide whether to finish the current setup or wait for the planned parts.
