WHAT IS THE CAR DOING?

Choose a starting point.

These guides explain what to check and why. They are not a diagnosis of your car or a set of values to copy into its ECU.

It cranks but won’t start

Start with engine speed and sync, then check whether the ECU is seeing the inputs it needs.

  • Understand trigger and sync loss
  • Link G4X first-start problems
  • Emtron trigger setup checks

Idle is unstable or throttle response is poor

Separate airflow, fuel delivery and control behavior before changing several tables at once.

  • Build a stable idle
  • Understand tip-in and transient fueling

Boost surges or keeps climbing

Boost oscillation and boost creep are different problems. Identify the behavior before adjusting control.

  • Boost that rises and falls
  • Boost that climbs with RPM
  • Wastegate control fundamentals

The throttle closes under load

Look at the ECU’s torque requests and limits, not just pedal position.

  • Why electronic throttles close
  • Understand torque intervention

A sensor or CAN device reads incorrectly

Check scaling, units and communication basics before treating a displayed value as a mechanical fault.

  • MAP sensor scaling
  • CAN wiring and termination
  • CAN message communication

I need a useful log or a tuning checklist

Get the right channels and setup information together before changing the calibration.

  • Read a datalog in context
  • Record and export Accessport logs
  • Vehicle and ECU preparation checklist

A misfire, abnormal noise, overheating or loss of fuel pressure is a reason to stop loaded testing and investigate, not keep making pulls for a cleaner log.
