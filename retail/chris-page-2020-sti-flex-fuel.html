THE SETUP

Early Green Speed flex fuel.
The original turbo.

This is a stock-turbo, bolt-on STI example. The April feature lists a COBB SF intake, Accessport and boost controller, plus exhaust, fuel-delivery and drivetrain work. HKS HiperMax coilovers were also part of the package.

A later AWD exhaust comparison documents Chris’s COBB catback and catted downpipe. AWD’s May 10 flex-fuel feature then records the NexGen Flex Fuel installation and tune on this 2020 STI.

The useful takeaway: tune choice follows the full combination. A factory turbo does not mean an otherwise stock car, and a flex-fuel calibration needs the appropriate hardware and compatibility review.
