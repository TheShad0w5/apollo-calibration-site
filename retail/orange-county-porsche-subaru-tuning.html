IN-PERSON ROAD TUNING / BY ARRANGEMENT

Plan the session. Leave time for the details.

Road tuning gives me time with the car to evaluate response, transient throttle behavior and how power comes in during real driving. The session is coordinated around your vehicle, its condition and the work we agree to complete.

  1. Send the setup first. Include your city, exact vehicle, ECU or tuning device, modifications, fuel, goals and any known issues.
  2. Agree on the day. We confirm scope, price, meeting or vehicle handover arrangements and testing requirements before you book.
  3. Plan to leave the car for the day. Arrange your own transportation. I confirm expected handover and collection times with you; the appointment is not a while-you-wait tune.
  4. Arrive ready. Complete the vehicle preparation checklist and bring the agreed fuel and equipment. Mechanical faults can change the session plan.

Road evaluation is kept within legal speeds and suitable conditions. Testing that needs higher loads or speeds is planned for a dyno or appropriate controlled facility.

Before you get started.

How long should I leave my car?

Plan to leave the car with me for the day and arrange transportation. We confirm handover, collection and the intended work in advance. Extra diagnostic or mechanical work can affect the plan.

Can we start without an in-person visit?

Yes. Send the setup and goals for an initial review. Whether the tuning itself can be completed remotely depends on the vehicle, ECU, condition and required testing.

What should I send?

Your city, year and model, engine and transmission, tuning device, installed parts, current fuel and any known problems. For a vintage Porsche, include the engine specification and throttle/ECU arrangement.

Can you tune at a dyno location I arrange?

Yes. Send the facility name, location, dyno type and proposed dates. We coordinate with the shop and agree on equipment, operator support, fees and the calibration plan before booking.

How do I choose between remote tuning and a dyno session?

The car’s readiness and the work needed guide that choice. Compare the two approaches, then send me your setup.

Tell me about your Porsche or Subaru.

Include “Orange County” and your city in your inquiry, along with your car and what you want to improve. I’ll help identify the appropriate tuning path.
