The planning lesson

Tell your tuner what fuel is available where you drive, how you use the car and whether this is the final setup or one stage in a larger build. Those details help frame the conversation before choosing a calibration path.

Questions to bring to Keith

  • What turbo and supporting hardware are already installed?
  • What fuel is currently in the car, and what will you use normally?
  • What ECU or tuning device is fitted, and who created the current calibration?
  • What response or drivability changes are you looking for?
  • Which upgrades are planned next?
