AIR-COOLED PORSCHE / RESTORATIONS & RECREATIONS

Preserve the character.
Refine the response.

My vintage Porsche experience includes supporting Kinsler products on 964-based 4.0-liter engines and consulting with Ed Pink Racing Engines on engine-management calibration. These projects bring the engine, throttle system, fuel delivery and ECU together as one working combination.

For a restoration or recreation, the conversation starts with how you want to use the car. A responsive road engine and a competition-focused combination can need different behavior. I consider the engine specification, drivetrain, tires and intended conditions alongside the power target.

Kinsler throttle systems

Calibration support for the installed induction and throttle arrangement, including individual throttle bodies. Cable linkage and drive-by-wire require different setup work; I establish which system is fitted before defining the scope.

Drive-by-wire integration

Pedal and throttle setup, response and the available control strategy are considered together. The aim is a predictable relationship between your input and the engine’s response.

Fueling & drivability

Injector configuration, starting, warm-up, idle and transient fueling are part of the work. Advanced fueling strategies are selected around the engine, sensors, fuel system and ECU capabilities.

PORSCHE TUNING FAQ

Choose the right route.

Can a vintage 964 use the same COBB service as a modern Porsche?

No. The vintage engines described here use a separately installed standalone ECU. Modern COBB tuning depends on a supported factory ECU and matching Accessport. Send the installed system details so I can identify the right service.

Do I need drive-by-wire for a Kinsler-equipped engine?

Not necessarily. My experience includes cable-throttle and DBW configurations. The appropriate setup depends on the installed hardware and your goals; a DBW conversion is a separate project, not an assumed part of a tune.

Can you support an engine builder or restoration shop?

Yes. I can work with your builder or shop on engine-management planning, calibration and dyno coordination. Send the specification and available facilities so we can define responsibilities and the session plan.

Does a COBB product listing guarantee you can custom-tune my car?

No. I confirm the exact year, trim, market, transmission, ECU software and device support before taking payment. PDK support and licensing are checked separately.

What will the engine make?

Output depends on the complete engine, fuel and test conditions. We agree on useful performance goals and evaluate the response and drivability as well as the dyno result.
