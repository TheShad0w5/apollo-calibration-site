What is COBB Green Speed?

Project Green Speed is COBB’s emissions-compliance initiative. It affects the hardware, maps and tuning tools available for supported vehicles. It does not mean that all Accessport tuning has ended.

The practical starting point is your exact vehicle, parts and fuel—not just a stage number. An OTS map needs to match its published requirements; a custom tune needs a supported combination that I can evaluate.

COBB’s Project Green Speed overview

What changed in April 2022?

Emissions controls

COBB removed Accesstuner adjustments affecting emissions-related diagnostics and systems, including certain trouble codes, oxygen sensors, catalysts, EGR, TGV and readiness settings.

Older flex-fuel methods

Some older analog Subaru and Nissan flex-fuel strategies depended on factory sensor inputs and diagnostic changes. Those methods were removed from the supported tuning software.

Editing an older map

Opening or revising an older calibration with current software may remove features or adjustments that are no longer supported. Review the setup before planning changes.

Read COBB’s April 2022 update

Can I still get a custom tune?

Yes. COBB’s FAQ explains that custom tuning remains available. The 2022 changes targeted Accesstuner, the tuner’s editing software; they did not erase maps already stored on an Accessport or end the device’s basic functionality.

That does not establish whether a particular old map suits your current parts or can be revised as expected. Share the existing map information and complete hardware list before a change.

COBB’s Green Speed FAQ

Is flex fuel still possible?

Yes, on supported combinations. COBB now offers CAN-based Subaru flex-fuel packages for specific applications. That is different from assuming an older analog kit still works with a new calibration.

Model year, market, ECU, Accessport, sensor kit and supporting fuel system all matter. Check the current product fitment and discuss the whole setup before buying parts.

Does this apply beyond Subaru?

I also accept custom-tuning inquiries for Nissan GT-R, Porsche, Ford and the other supported COBB makes. The available features and hardware requirements vary by application; Subaru-specific flex-fuel details should not be applied to another platform.

Before you buy a map.

  • Send the model year, engine, transmission and vehicle market.
  • Include the Accessport part number and current map details.
  • List all modifications, including intake, exhaust and fuel-system parts.
  • Specify the fuel you will actually use and any ethanol-content equipment.
  • Mention warning lights, mechanical issues and planned changes.

A custom tune is not a substitute for repairing a mechanical fault. Green Speed compatibility alone is not a blanket claim that every vehicle, part or custom map has a particular emissions certification.

This guide summarizes COBB’s published information, checked October 1, 2026. Product support can change; the linked manufacturer information governs current fitment.
