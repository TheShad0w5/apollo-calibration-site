What a dyno session can cover.

A dyno provides a controlled environment for observing how the engine responds under load. I review the available data alongside the vehicle’s hardware, fuel and intended use, then refine the calibration where appropriate.

  • Fueling, ignition and boost calibration for supported systems.
  • Power delivery and repeatability across the usable operating range.
  • Review of sensor behavior, temperature trends and ECU interventions.
  • Investigation of calibration concerns that need testing under load.

Peak power is one result of the process. Starting behavior, response and a calibration appropriate to the complete build matter too.

E-tuning or an in-person dyno session?

Remote e-tuning suits a supported, mechanically sound vehicle when you can provide the requested logs and follow the test plan. We work through the calibration in revisions, using your data and feedback.

An in-person dyno session brings the vehicle, operator and calibration work together for controlled load testing. It can be useful for a new combination, standalone ECU development or a concern that needs direct observation. The right choice depends on the car’s condition, equipment and goals.

If you are unsure, send me the setup first. I’ll help identify the next step before you arrange dyno time or travel.
