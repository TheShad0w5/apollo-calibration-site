LUCAS BAMFORD / 2018 STI / EMTRON

My idea of the ultimate GT STI.

Lucas’s STI is a good example of what I mean by the complete car. Its Emtron calibration and three SI-Drive power modes give him different levels of performance with smooth drivability as a priority.

The E80 dyno report recorded 564.5 hp and 550.2 lb-ft. Lucas also reported a trouble-free 700-mile drive home and praised the smooth ride. Those are different parts of the same story.

CINDERELLI / 2024 WRX / E70

Developing the whole package.

Cinderelli’s documented development connects tuning with the hardware supporting it. AWD’s May 2026 update records 388 whp and 417 wtq on E70 alongside a new 240 mm XClutch installation.

It’s another example of why I consider the complete setup when discussing a car’s next step.

CHRIS PAGE / 2020 STI / STOCK TURBO

Bolt-ons, flex fuel and the first drive.

One of our early Green Speed-compliant flex-fuel builds at AWD Tuning, retaining the original turbo. Hear Chris’s first-drive reaction and follow the separate pump-gas and ethanol sessions.

The May 2023 E75 summary records 334.9 hp and 387.9 lb-ft, with the dated reports and setup explained in the case study.

CHRIS T / 2016 WRX / FA20DIT

A developed IAG and Kelford build.

An IAG shortblock, Kelford cams and extensive supporting upgrades. Explore the original chart and separate 2025 and 2026 development records.

The May 2026 individual summaries record 351.3 hp on 93 octane and 431.8 hp on E70. These are separate pulls with their own test context.
