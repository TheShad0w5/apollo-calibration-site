01 / MY APPROACH

The driving experience
comes first.

Before I start tuning, I want to understand where the car needs to perform and what you expect from it.

A street car and a competition car have different demands. The right power level depends on the complete setup—including the suspension, tires, available grip and mechanical limits.

I pay close attention to transient throttle response: how the engine reacts as you get on the pedal, ease off and get back into it. The relationship between throttle input and boost matters just as much as peak output. Power should arrive in a way you can predict and use.

Those details shape how confidently you can drive the car, whether you’re enjoying it on the street or trying to be competitive at an event. A dyno number tells part of the story. The experience behind the wheel tells me much more.
