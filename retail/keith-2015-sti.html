KEITH’S OWN CAR / 2015 SUBARU STI

Built by Keith.
Developed over years.

My white 2015 STI makes 750 whp with a Precision 6466. It connects my engine-building work with my tuning: a built EJ257, years of turbo development and a later Emtron chapter. The archive follows the car through its changing configurations.

A street car with competition in mind.

AWD’s 2016 turbo-development post describes my goal: a daily-driven STI with a useful power band, built around the requirements of the FIS class for events such as Import Face-Off. That balance gives the project its identity—hardware, response and the intended use considered together.

This is my own white 2015 STI. The Mexico STI, the older Texas Mile cars and other customers’ white Subarus are separate builds.

The useful part of a long-running project is seeing how the decisions connect: engine construction, turbo choice, fuel delivery, drivetrain work and calibration. Each stage starts with the hardware actually on the car.

THE CAR OVER TIME

Different stages. One STI.

February 26, 2016 · Built EJ257 first start

AWD’s start-up video identifies my 2015 STI and documents its Stage 4 engine, Garrett GTX3582 and ETS turbo system. COBB Accessport tuning on E85 was planned, with a stated target of 575 whp.

The channel’s subsequent comment reports 644 whp and 567 wtq on E85 at 32 psi. That is a reported result for this early configuration, separate from later turbo and ECU development.

August 22, 2016 · Precision 6270 direction

A dated post names my new Precision 6270, with a 0.96 A/R and T4/v-band-outlet description. The combination retained the ETS turbo kit, FIC 2150 cc injectors and Radium fuel system, with a COBB exhaust mentioned for street use. The post explains the planned class fit and daily-driving goals; it does not publish a completed dyno result for that turbo.

DOCUMENTED FEBRUARY 2016 CONFIGURATION

The engine behind the early build.

AWD Stage 4 EJ257

  • Darton sleeves, JE pistons and Pauter rods
  • Stock STI heat-treated crankshaft
  • Built cylinder heads and Kelford 282 camshafts

Air, fuel & supporting hardware

  • Garrett GTX3582 and ETS turbo kit
  • ETS front-mount intercooler and headers
  • Fuel Injector Clinic 2150 cc injectors
  • IAG fuel rails and Radium surge tank
  • COBB boost-control solenoid
  • Invidia Q300 exhaust and IAG air/oil separator

These parts describe the original video’s configuration. The later Precision turbo and Emtron stages are recorded separately above.

POWER & DEVELOPMENT

750 whp. Precision 6466.

My STI went on to a Precision 6466 and makes 750 whp. The earlier results below document its previous Garrett configuration, rather than the later turbo combination.
