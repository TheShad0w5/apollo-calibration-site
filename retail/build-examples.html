2019 Subaru STI — BCP turbo on 93 octane

Read this build story →

I tuned Zach’s 2019 STI with a BCP turbo on 93-octane fuel—a relatively simple combination focused on a strong, usable power band.

The tuning takeaway: A turbo package should suit the fuel you actually use and the response you want, as well as your longer-term plans.

Subaru WRX — FP Green on E85

Chris’s FP Green WRX needed fuel-delivery issues resolved before I could tune its E85 setup on the dyno. The project shows why preparation matters as much as the parts list.

The tuning takeaway: Fuel-system readiness comes before calibration. An inspection can identify hardware problems that a tune alone cannot solve.

Original project media: AWD Tuning.

Watch the original WRX video ↗Discuss a similar setup
2024 Subaru BRZ — headers and retuning

This 2024 BRZ came in for a header installation and my calibration work. It appears alongside two other BRZs in the original AWD Tuning shop feature; look for the car identified as the 2024 “BURZ.”

The tuning takeaway: Calibration work extends beyond turbocharged cars. Match the tuning approach to the exact vehicle and its installed hardware.

Original project media: AWD Tuning.

See the original BRZ shop feature ↗
Subaru STI — intercooler development and dyno tuning

In a customer post shared on AWD Tuning’s profile, @feistii_rach documents an R&D installation of PERRIN’s extra-large-core front-mount intercooler. With other modifications also planned, the car received a base map after installation and a dyno tune after the remaining changes were complete.

The tuning takeaway: Plan the calibration around the complete modification list. The owner specifically notes that the intercooler alone did not require a tune.

Owner account: @feistii_rach, shared by AWD Tuning.
