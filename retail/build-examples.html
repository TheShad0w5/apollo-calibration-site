Every build starts with a different question.

The fuel, hardware and intended use shape the answer. These historical examples draw on AWD Tuning’s social archive, dyno records, owner accounts and my firsthand build history. Sources are identified with each feature; they are not presented as new Calibrations LLC customer projects.

02 / FUEL DELIVERY & DYNO PREPARATION

Subaru WRX — FP Green on E85

Chris’s WRX arrived with an FP Green turbo and an E85 setup. The pre-dyno inspection identified fuel-delivery issues. AWD Tuning reports resolving those issues before the car’s dyno runs.

03 / 388 WHP / 417 WTQ • REPORTED ON E70

2024 Subaru WRX “Cinderelli” — E70

Read this build story →

AWD Tuning’s post documents Cinderelli’s 2024 WRX setup at 388 whp and 417 wtq on E70. It also records installation of an XClutch 240 mm single-plate clutch, connecting the car’s power level with its drivetrain requirements.

The tuning takeaway: A usable performance package considers the drivetrain alongside the engine calibration. These are the figures reported for this car, not a promised result for another build.

05 / HARDWARE DEVELOPMENT & CALIBRATION

Subaru STI — intercooler development and dyno tuning

In a customer post shared on AWD Tuning’s profile, @feistii_rach documents an R&D installation of PERRIN’s extra-large-core front-mount intercooler. With other modifications also planned, the car received a base map after installation and a dyno tune after the remaining changes were complete.

The tuning takeaway: Plan the calibration around the complete modification list. The owner specifically notes that the intercooler alone did not require a tune.

06 / STANDALONE ECU • DOCUMENTED PROJECT PLAN

2005 Subaru WRX — Haltech engine-management plan

AWD Tuning outlines a Haltech Elite 2500 package for Antonio’s 2005 WRX: a WB1 wideband controller, flex-fuel composition sensor, CAN hub and keypad. The post also names a Garrett G30-900 turbo and Kelford cams, and tags me in the tuning plans.

The tuning takeaway: This source documents the planned package, rather than a completed dyno result. It shows the engine-management decisions behind a build intended to accommodate both 93 octane and E85.

FEATURED HALTECH BUILD / 2004 SUBARU STI

Thomas’s STI: 767.3 hp.

Thomas Rodriguez’s 2004 Subaru WRX STI documents another chapter of my Haltech tuning work at AWD Tuning, with dyno records spanning December 2024 and July 2025.

767.3 hp · 562.2 lb-ft

July 8, 2025 · EFR 9280 · 34 psi

The earlier December 27, 2024 report records 679.5 hp and 534.9 lb-ft, with the file identifying the HT/E80 configuration. The later report identifies an EFR 9280 setup at 34 psi. I confirm the later graph’s 767.3 hp and 562.2 lb-ft figures as the correct result.

Source: AWD Tuning’s Mustang dynamometer records and Keith Fields’ confirmation. Historical AWD Tuning work. Fuel for the July run is not specified here; results describe this car’s tested configurations.
