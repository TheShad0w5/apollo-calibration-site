FACTORY ECU TUNING

What is EcuTek?

EcuTek provides software and interfaces for tuning supported factory ECUs. The route available to you depends on the car, ECU and chosen programming method.

Where it fits

Keith’s EcuTek work includes Subaru BRZ and Toyota GR86 applications. Tell me the exact vehicle before choosing hardware, licenses or a tuning service.

What to send Keith

  • Model year, market, engine and transmission.
  • Existing EcuTek interface, license and current calibration details.
  • Intake, exhaust, forced-induction and fuel-system modifications.

Before your tuning session

The interface, software and license need to match your vehicle and tuning workflow. ECU Connect is EcuTek’s companion app for compatible interfaces; programming support varies by application. I will confirm the equipment before we start.
