FACTORY ECU TUNING

What is EcuTek?

EcuTek provides software and interfaces for tuning supported factory ECUs. The route available to you depends on the car, ECU and chosen programming method.

Software and logging

ProECU and ECU Connect serve different roles. ProECU is part of EcuTek’s computer-based workflow; ECU Connect is the mobile app used with a compatible Bluetooth interface. PhoneFlash programming is available for supported applications through an agreed tuner workflow.

The interface, ECU license and any PhoneFlash entitlement need to match the vehicle. Having the app or cable alone does not establish that the car is ready to receive a tune.

What about RaceROM?

RaceROM adds features for supported applications. Available functions depend on the vehicle, software and calibration; ask about the specific feature you want rather than assuming every EcuTek car has it.

For review, send the original log from the agreed app or software, the tune revision and a brief description of the test.

ECU Connect kits, flash points and PhoneFlash

Before buying anything, check which interface you already have and whether your ECU has an EcuTek license. A kit listing can bundle hardware, a license or tuning, so check each item against your quote.

ECU Connect Bluetooth interface or ProECU kit?

  • ECU Connect: Use the EcuTek EVI-BT interface in the car’s OBD-II port with the ECU Connect app on iPhone, iPad or Android. It can read ECU information, capture logs and read fault codes. A regular Bluetooth OBD-II reader cannot substitute for the EVI-BT.
  • ProECU: The Windows workflow uses a supported EcuTek interface and ProECU license key. Older retail kits typically include a wired interface and a separate USB license key. Some EVI-BT interfaces can also connect by USB for ProECU. Check your exact hardware before ordering.

How PhoneFlash works

Pair the EVI-BT in ECU Connect, sign in, select your tuner and share the vehicle’s ECU details. Your tuner prepares the calibration and uploads it to EcuTek Cloud. ECU Connect downloads the file, then writes it to the ECU over Bluetooth on supported cars. With ProECU, you load the supplied file on a Windows computer and program over a cable. App logging and RaceROM map switching are separate functions.

Before programming, confirm the file and ECU ID with your tuner and connect a suitable battery support supply. Follow the app’s ignition prompts, keep the phone nearby and leave the app open until the flash finishes. EcuTek PhoneFlash setup guide
