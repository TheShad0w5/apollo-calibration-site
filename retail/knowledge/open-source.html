THREE DIFFERENT JOBS

EcuFlash, RomRaider and the interface.

“Open-source tuning” describes a commonly used workflow, not one universal product. RomRaider is open-source software. EcuFlash and the hardware have their own distribution and licensing terms. Installing the software does not establish compatibility with your ECU.

READ & WRITE

EcuFlash

Used for supported ECU reading and reflashing, with calibration editing available through matching definitions. The exact ECU and interface determine which operations are supported.

EDIT & LOG

RomRaider

A free, open-source editor and datalogger. ECU definitions describe calibration tables; logger definitions describe the channels that can be recorded. Those are separate pieces of the setup.

CONNECT

Vehicle interface

The bridge between the computer and ECU. An OpenPort is an interface, not a replacement ECU. A cable that connects to the diagnostic port is not automatically suitable for reflashing.

THE ROM MATTERS

Will it work on my WRX or STI?

Start with the actual ECU, not just the badge on the trunk. This route can suit supported older Subaru factory ECUs, but model year alone cannot establish reading, editing, logging and flashing support. Engine swaps, replacement ECUs and an unknown previous tune need additional review.

Send these details

  • Year, model, market and transmission.
  • Engine or ECU swaps and current hardware.
  • ECU identification and ROM/calibration ID, if available.
  • Current tune history, interface and software versions.
  • Fuel grade, measured ethanol content if relevant, and your goals.

What I check

  • Whether the ECU and ROM have suitable definitions.
  • Whether your equipment supports the required operations.
  • How we will capture useful engine and wideband data.
  • Mechanical concerns that need attention first.
  • The tuning scope and price before you commit.

A working logger is only one part of the answer.

Being able to see live data does not prove that the ROM can be safely edited or reflashed. Keep the untouched original read and its identifying information. Never substitute a file from a similar-looking car.

USEFUL DATA / CLEAR FEEDBACK

What does a RomRaider datalog tell us?

A log lets me compare the engine’s operating conditions with its response. I choose the channel list and test conditions for your ECU and the current revision. More selected channels are not automatically better: the useful sampling rate and available parameters depend on the setup.

Operating conditions

Examples include engine speed, throttle position, calculated load or airflow, manifold pressure, intake-air temperature and coolant temperature.

Fueling and response

Depending on support, requested channels may include fuel corrections, injector activity, ignition timing, knock-related corrections and external wideband AFR or lambda.

These are channel categories, not a universal list to select blindly. ECU names and units vary. A dashboard photo or a wideband gauge video does not replace the requested time-based data.

Send the original log, not a screenshot.

Keep the exported file intact. Include the revision name, fuel, conditions and what you noticed. If the wideband records separately, follow the agreed method for aligning the data. Stop testing when the car behaves abnormally and tell me what happened.

Common open-source tuning questions.

Is the tuning free because the software is free?

No. Software availability and professional calibration are different things. My service covers reviewing your combination, developing the calibration and working through the agreed logging and revision process. Equipment and any applicable licensing are separate unless stated in your quote.

Can you work with a car that already has a tune?

Send the tuning history and ECU information first. An existing file may have unknown changes or protections. I need to establish what is in the car and what is supported before promising a read or revision.

Do I need both EcuFlash and RomRaider?

The required tools depend on the ECU and the work. They commonly handle complementary jobs, but I will confirm the actual workflow instead of asking you to install every available program.

Can I use the factory oxygen sensor instead of a wideband?

For the EJ remote fueling work described here, an external wideband with an approved logging method is required. We need the right measurement at the operating conditions being evaluated, not just a dashboard reading.

Can I send logs from public-road pulls?

Follow the agreed logging plan in appropriate, controlled conditions. Do not operate a laptop while driving. Where the required testing cannot be performed appropriately, arrange a suitable dyno session.

Atlas: a separate WRX tuning option

I also offer Atlas tuning for supported FA20 and FA24 WRX setups. Current Atlas software is developed by North American Motorsports Research and is no longer open source. It has its own compatibility, adapter and licensing requirements.

Before choosing Atlas

  • Send the year, market, transmission, ECU/ROM ID and current tune history.
  • Confirm the Atlas version and supported adapter before buying equipment.
  • Tell me about your hardware, fuel and goals so we can agree on logging and revision scope.

Atlas cannot use an Accessport as its flashing or logging interface, and it does not edit Accessport tune files. Plan any platform change with me first; do not return a modified car to stock calibration without checking that it is appropriate for its hardware.

STI/EJ support is not currently available as a service here. For those cars, ask about the appropriate COBB, EcuFlash/RomRaider or standalone route.
