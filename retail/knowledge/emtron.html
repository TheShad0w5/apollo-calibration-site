THE INSTALLATION COMES FIRST

Make the strategy match the car.

Response and power delivery

Describe the driving you actually do: street use, track sessions or a specific competition format. We can discuss throttle response, boost delivery and selectable modes where the installed system supports them. The useful result is a car that responds predictably, not just a peak dyno number.

Inputs before interventions

Pressure, temperature, lambda and engine-position signals need to be correctly wired and calibrated before they can support useful control or protection. Send sensor part numbers and the wiring record. Protection thresholds are developed for the engine and its operating conditions; they are not a universal preset.

Integration and capacity

List the throttle, dash, power controller and other CAN devices. We review which unit controls each function and what information must be shared. KV, SL and Shadow hardware should be identified by exact model rather than treated as interchangeable.

Where it fits

It is a route for developed engine projects, conversions and builds that need a different control strategy from the factory ECU. My Emtron projects include Lucas’s STI and Porsche engine builds.

Software and logging

Emtune is used to inspect the configuration, tune the ECU and review data. It supports live PC logging while connected to the car, as well as retrieving supported onboard ECU recordings.

A useful review includes the working calibration and an original log that captures the symptom. For example, a cold-start problem needs data from the start, not just a screenshot after the engine has warmed up. I will specify the channels and test conditions.

Why include the sensor and wiring details?

The tune relies on correctly configured temperature, pressure, throttle and engine-position signals. A number on the screen is only useful if the input and calibration match the installed sensor.

What to send Keith

  • ECU model, firmware and current calibration.
  • Engine specification, crank/cam trigger system and wiring diagram.
  • Sensor list, fuel system, throttle setup and CAN-connected devices, such as a dash or power controller.

If you are unsure which system you have, start with a photo of the ECU label and the details you know.

Before your tuning session

Bring the current configuration and logs, plus a clear account of any starting, idle or driving issue. Emtron’s Emtune software and resource centre provide the platform-specific configuration and logging reference.

Make the session productive.

  1. Send the existing record. Include the ECU label, current tune and logs, engine specification, wiring information and a description of the goal or fault.
  2. Agree on the scope. We distinguish installation checks, first-start work, drivability refinement and dyno calibration. Hardware, software access and unresolved faults can change the plan.
  3. Prepare the vehicle. Resolve leaks, misfires and known mechanical concerns. Confirm fuel, battery condition and the logging equipment. Follow the vehicle preparation checklist before the appointment.
  4. Review the result. Keep the agreed final file and instructions. Let me know before changing the hardware, sensors or fuel afterward.

Emtron planning questions.

Can an existing Emtron tune be refined?

Yes, subject to reviewing the installed system, file access and current condition. Tell me what works, what does not, and which hardware or fuel has changed. Keep the original calibration and provide a log that captures the concern before we plan changes.

Can you supply hardware as well as tune it?

I offer ECU sales and project packages. Send your engine, intended use and existing equipment so we can confirm the hardware, accessories and calibration scope before quoting. Product photos are examples, not promises of stock or fitment.

Can this be handled remotely?

That depends on the installation, the work needed and the available data. Send the project details first. An unresolved wiring or mechanical issue may require hands-on checks before calibration can progress.

Can I use a dyno I have arranged?

Yes, subject to coordination. Confirm the location, equipment, operator, schedule and scope with me before booking. Travel and in-person work require planning and are quoted for the project.

Plan the next step

Tell me about your car, the equipment already installed and what you want to improve. I can review the setup before you order a tune or buy more parts.
