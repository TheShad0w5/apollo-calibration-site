STANDALONE ENGINE MANAGEMENT

What is Link ECU?

Link ECU makes standalone engine-management systems. A project may use a vehicle-specific plug-in ECU or a wired installation, depending on the engine and chassis.

Where it fits

The starting point is the exact ECU model and its supported functions. Plug-in fitment still needs a review of the car’s wiring, sensors and modifications.

Software and logging

PCLink is the configuration, tuning and log-review software. Choose the release for the exact ECU generation; a G4+, G4X or newer system should not be identified only as “a Link.” Link maintains current and legacy software information.

Send the current tune and an original log with the ECU model, firmware and PCLink version. A log records what the engine did; the tune explains how the ECU was configured at the time.

Plug-in or wire-in?

A plug-in ECU is designed around a specific application’s connections. A wire-in ECU uses a separately built or adapted harness. Both still need the correct sensor setup, pin assignments and engine configuration, especially after hardware or engine swaps.

What to send Keith

  • ECU model and generation, firmware and calibration file.
  • Whether the installation is plug-in or wire-in.
  • Engine, trigger, fuel-system and throttle specifications, plus logs.

If you are unsure which system you have, start with a photo of the ECU label and the details you know.

Before your tuning session

Confirm the correct PCLink software and connection method for the ECU generation. Share the existing calibration and a short description of the issue or goal so I can plan the session.
