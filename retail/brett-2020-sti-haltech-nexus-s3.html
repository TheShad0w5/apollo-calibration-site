THE PROJECT

Keep the focus on the drive.

Brett’s STI was previously on an Accessport. He had experienced recurring P0420 faults, including with the stock downpipe fitted, and opted for a different engine-management solution.

The aim was a well-driving STI with minimal intrusion. ECU selection and calibration were part of that project; the dyno figures document the tuning sessions, rather than a diagnosis or repair of the catalyst fault.

MARCH 5, 2026 · AWD PROJECT ARCHIVE

A response-focused turbo plan.

AWD’s named post for Brett’s 2020 STI documents a COBB TD05H-20G prepared for installation, with low- and mid-range response part of the upgrade’s intended appeal.

The post records that stage of the project; it does not establish the installed hardware for every later tuning session.
