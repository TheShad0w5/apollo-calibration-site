BEFORE ORDERING

Start with the right information.

Vehicle and tuning tools

US-market 2002–2005 WRX EJ205, 2006–2014 WRX EJ255 and 2004–2021 STI EJ257 are the core applications here. Engine, ECU, wiring and transmission swaps require individual review. Supported COBB and open-source workflows depend on exact ROM and software compatibility.

Your hardware and fuel

Stock-location and rotated turbos, built engines, injectors, fuel pressure regulation and boost-control changes all affect the plan. Include exact parts and fuel, plus tire, suspension and intended-use details where relevant.

Logging and mechanical readiness

EJ remote tuning requires an external wideband with an approved way to record AFR or lambda alongside the engine log. A factory oxygen-sensor reading, gauge display or gauge video does not replace that recorded data. Confirm the controller and logging connection with me before buying equipment.

Tell me about any misfires, leaks, warning lights or other known faults before we start. I may ask for mechanical checks before tuning. Follow the logging instructions supplied for your car.

WHAT TO EXPECT

How your e-tune works.

  1. Confirm the setup. Choose the service for your car, fuel and tuning device. Ask me first if compatibility or the work needed is uncertain.
  2. Order and complete onboarding. Pay through secure QuickBooks checkout, then provide the vehicle, hardware and fuel details needed to prepare your calibration.
  3. Log, review and refine. Follow the instructions for each file. Upload the requested logs and driving feedback under Messages, logs & revision history on your private order page. I review the data and return revisions. See how your order page works →
  4. Finish with the approved file. I confirm when the tuning process is complete. Follow the instructions supplied with each map, including any limits during development.

What the revision allowance covers

Most custom e-tunes include up to eight calibration revisions for the approved setup. Each revision follows a review of usable logs and feedback; downloading the same file again does not use a revision. Changing hardware or fuel, diagnosing faults or needing extra revisions can change the scope. I discuss any additional work and cost with you first.

Typical turnaround

Initial files and revisions are usually sent the same day, or within one business day once I have the approved setup, required information or logs, and any payment due. Business hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Pacific. The full tuning process depends on your logging, feedback and the car’s condition.

BEFORE YOU BUY / CHOOSE YOUR TUNE

Which tuning service fits my Subaru?

Should I choose a single-fuel or flex-fuel tune?

The listed Accessport services are $400 for single fuel and $600 for flex fuel. Tell me what fuel you can consistently obtain and the exact fuel-system and ethanol-sensing hardware installed. A flex-fuel selection does not establish that your ECU, software and hardware are compatible. I confirm the combination and scope before tuning. Hardware, licenses and diagnostic work may be separate. Compare the listed services →

What if I change parts or fuel during tuning?

Contact me before changing the agreed hardware or fuel. Send the new part details or fuel plan and your current revision so I can confirm whether the existing file still suits the car. Do not assume a previous calibration covers the change.

The included revisions apply to the approved setup and scope. Hardware or fuel changes, diagnostics and extra revisions may require additional work; we agree on that scope and any cost before continuing. See the tuning process and preparation guide before collecting your next logs.

Does my car qualify for a retune?

The listed Accessport retunes are for an existing Keith calibration: $200 single fuel or $300 flex fuel. Include your previous order, current file and every hardware or fuel change. If the car has another tuner's calibration, or you do not know its history, ask me to review it before selecting a retune. I confirm whether the change fits the retune service or needs a different scope.

Do I need an Accessport, or can I use open-source tools?

Send the exact ECU/ROM, current calibration and interface details before buying a device or selecting a service. Supported COBB and open-source workflows are reviewed for the installed ECU and software. An Accessport tune file or license is not interchangeable with an open-source file. Engine and ECU swaps need their own review. Ask which route fits my EJ →

Does my older flex-fuel kit mean I should select the flex-fuel service?

Not by itself. Send the kit brand and part number, controller, wiring, ECU/ROM and current software. Legacy hardware does not prove that a current COBB software version can edit or use the installed configuration. Review the Green Speed compatibility guide and confirm the supported route with me before ordering or changing anything.

What if I cannot record the wideband data you request?

Tell me before choosing remote tuning. For EJ WRX/STI remote tuning, an external wideband and an approved recording method are required. A gauge video is not a replacement for the requested data. We can discuss the logging setup or a coordinated dyno session with suitable instrumentation before proceeding.
