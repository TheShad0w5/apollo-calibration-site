THE PERSON BEHIND THE TUNE

Keith Fields.
A history in Subaru performance.

Keith has been tuning since 2005. His work through AWD Tuning connects the early Subaru open-source community with years of dyno development, competition builds and hands-on troubleshooting.

That background is still visible in the community’s archives. RomRaider’s Professional Tuner Directory lists Keith Fields and AWDTuning in its Central US section, describing Subaru open-source tuning, dyno tuning, remote tuning and engine-management work. See Keith’s RomRaider listing ↗.

In his DFW Subies interview, Keith describes leaving a career in IT to build a business where he could take personal responsibility for the work. He recalls buying his Subaru in 2005, spending years testing combinations and calibrations, then building a following through word of mouth and tuning trips. That demand eventually led to a dedicated shop. Hear Keith tell the story, starting at 26:49 ↗.

For Keith, tuning means understanding the complete vehicle: its hardware, fuel, mechanical condition and the way its owner intends to use it. Peak output is one part of the work. Drivability, useful response and a clear conversation about the build matter too.

At Calibrations LLC, you can discuss your setup directly with Keith, whether you need a custom Subaru tune, supported COBB Accessport e-tuning, standalone ECU calibration or help diagnosing a difficult engine-management problem.
