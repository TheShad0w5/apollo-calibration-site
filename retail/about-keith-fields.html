THE PERSON BEHIND THE TUNE

Keith Fields.

Subaru roots. Worldwide experience.

Over 20 years of experience. Attention to every detail.

I’ve been tuning since 2005, building my reputation through hands-on development, straightforward advice and a commitment to understanding the complete vehicle.

After leaving a career in IT to establish AWD Tuning, I grew the business through word of mouth, tuning trips and lasting relationships with automotive enthusiasts all over the globe. What began with my passion for honing my own Subaru developed into more than two decades of work spanning street cars, competition builds, engine development and advanced engine management.

I approach each project with curiosity and care. I look beyond the peak number on a dyno graph to consider how the engine starts, how power arrives and how the car behaves in everyday use. Hardware, fuel quality, mechanical condition and the owner’s goals all shape the process of each individual tune.

That experience includes developing AWD Tuning’s engine program, working with standalone systems and supporting builds across a wide range of performance levels. I bring the same attention to a customer’s first modifications as I do to a complex, high-output project—and take the time to explain what the setup needs and why.

My roots remain documented in the Subaru community. You can find my name in RomRaider’s Professional Tuner Directory, while my DFW Subies interview follows my path from early experimentation to building an established tuning business, AWD Tuning. My involvement in the industry extends beyond the shop, with ties to the broader automotive aftermarket community through SEMA dating back over a decade.

My Porsche work includes modern COBB calibration and vintage 964 engine projects using Kinsler throttle systems with MaxxECU, Emtron Shadow and MoTeC. Alongside Subaru tuning, I support standalone ECU setup, calibration and packages for owners, restoration shops and engine builders.

When you’re Tuned by Keith, you work directly with me. Whether you need a custom tune, supported COBB Accessport e-tuning, standalone ECU calibration or help resolving an engine-management problem, you get experienced guidance and a clear conversation about your build.

For the businesses connected with this work, explore the shops and affiliates, including AWD Tuning and Ed Pink Racing Engines. If your project involves a shop, include its name and location so we can coordinate the calibration work.

Tell me about your setup and what you want to achieve today!
