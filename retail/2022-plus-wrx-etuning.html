BEFORE ORDERING

Start with the right information.

Vehicle and tuning tools

Send the exact model year, market, transmission, Accessport part number and current map. The VB uses the FA24 engine; support is confirmed for your specific ECU and software. “2022+” does not guarantee every future model year.

Your hardware and fuel

Identify the exact intake and MAF housing, intercooler, charge piping, exhaust, turbo and fuel-system changes. ETS, Perrin and COBB are brands, not complete compatibility specifications: include part numbers and all mixed-brand components.

Logging and mechanical readiness

I confirm the channels and measurement equipment required for your exact setup before logging begins. Include your fuel grade or measured ethanol content and any known faults. A 2018+ model year does not automatically mean no additional measurement equipment is needed.

Known misfires, leaks, warning lights or other mechanical issues need to be disclosed. I may request checks before tuning proceeds. Collect logs only as instructed in appropriate, controlled conditions.

WHAT TO EXPECT

One setup. A clear tuning process.

  1. Choose your service: select the listed tune that fits your setup. If compatibility or scope is uncertain, send a free inquiry before ordering.
  2. Checkout and onboarding: provide your details and pay through the secure QuickBooks invoice. Quick checkout collects your name and email; complete vehicle, hardware and fuel details are still needed before tuning begins.
  3. Log review and revisions: return the requested data and driving feedback through the delivery method agreed during onboarding. I use that information to develop the calibration.
  4. Completion: follow the instructions supplied with your final file. A base map or intermediate revision is not a completed tune.

BEFORE YOU BUY / CHOOSE YOUR TUNE

Which tuning service fits my Subaru?

Should I choose a single-fuel or flex-fuel tune?

The listed Accessport services are $400 for single fuel and $600 for flex fuel. Tell me what fuel you can consistently obtain and the exact fuel-system and ethanol-sensing hardware installed. A flex-fuel selection does not establish that your ECU, software and hardware are compatible. I confirm the combination and scope before tuning. Hardware, licenses and diagnostic work may be separate. Compare the listed services →

Does my car qualify for a retune?

The listed Accessport retunes are for an existing Keith calibration: $200 single fuel or $300 flex fuel. Include your previous order, current file and every hardware or fuel change. If the car has another tuner's calibration, or you do not know its history, ask me to review it before selecting a retune. I confirm whether the change fits the retune service or needs a different scope.

Can I choose a tune just by the intake brand or a Stage 1 / Stage 2 label?

No. Send the intake and MAF-housing part numbers, plus the intercooler, charge pipes, turbo, exhaust and fuel-system details. Mixed-brand combinations need the same review. A stage label or an ETS, Perrin or COBB name is not a complete map specification. Read what stage labels mean, then describe the parts actually installed.

Does the same service cover manual and CVT WRXs?

Send the transmission, exact model year, market, Accessport part number and current software before ordering. I confirm support for that combination; the 2022+ heading is not a promise that every transmission, ECU or future model year is supported. Engine tuning and any transmission work must be scoped separately.

Should I copy the fuel used in the Cinderelli or James build?

Use those projects to discuss your goals, not to choose fuel or load a file by analogy. The cars have their own hardware and development stages. Tell me what is in your tank now, what you can obtain locally and which parts are installed. We agree on a plan for your car; another WRX's result is not a promised output.
