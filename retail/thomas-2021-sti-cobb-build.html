THE COMBINATION

More than a turbo swap.

The archived build specification pairs an IAG Stage 2.5 closed-deck engine with Kelford 280/282 cams and the Garrett turbo. An ACT twin-disc clutch supports the drivetrain side of the project. The combination was set up for 93-octane and E85 fuel.

AWD’s original public feature identifies the car as a built 2021 STI and names the IAG closed-deck engine, ETS turbo kit and GTX3576R. Its installed-turbo photograph also appears in Thomas’s named project archive.

The earlier AWD build graphic reported 575 whp and 470 lb-ft. This represents an early stage of the project, not its final output.

Later development

I recall Thomas’s STI later reaching approximately 611 whp. The dated sheet for that later session is being matched to the archive; the earlier torque figure is not being carried over to that result.
