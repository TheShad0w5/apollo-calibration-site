Looking for a tuner?

Whether you call it a tune, an e-tune, a pro tune or ECU calibration, the starting point is your vehicle and what you want it to do. Work with Keith on Subaru WRX and STI tuning, dyno tuning, or standalone ECU tuning.

Remote e-tuning is available for supported COBB Accessport applications. Other COBB-compatible vehicles and standalone projects are assessed by exact model, ECU and installed hardware.

TUNING, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Tuning or calibration?

Tuning is what most enthusiasts call it. Calibration is the technical work behind the tune. In ECU tuning, the words often describe the same work.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Do I need to know which service to choose?

No. Share the vehicle, ECU or tuning device, modifications and your goals. Keith will help identify whether COBB tuning, standalone support, diagnostics or a dyno session is the appropriate starting point.

Can I work with Keith as an individual owner?

Yes. Apollo welcomes individual owners as well as shops, engine builders and teams. You do not need a complete build sheet to start the conversation.

How are pricing and turnaround determined?

Scope, required information, pricing, availability and any revision or follow-up support are discussed before work begins. The process depends on the vehicle and service.
