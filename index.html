BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Can you e-tune my vehicle?

Compatibility depends on the model year, engine, ECU, tuning device and modifications. The platforms listed above describe experience, not blanket support for every vehicle. Send your details to confirm fit.

What equipment do I need?

You’ll need a supported way to flash your ECU and record the requested data. Additional equipment depends on your setup. Confirm requirements with Keith before purchasing anything.

How much does an e-tune cost?

Request a quote for your setup. Pricing, revision scope, expected timing and any follow-up support are agreed before tuning begins.

Can tuning fix a mechanical problem?

A tune cannot repair a mechanical fault. If your vehicle has an unresolved issue, include it in your inquiry so the right diagnostic steps can come first.
