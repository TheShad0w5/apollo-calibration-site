Affirm / Intuit checkout integration — November 4, 2026

Affirm is embedded in QuickBooks Payments. Do not add a separate Affirm SDK,
collect financing applications on the TBK website or promise buyer approval.
Intuit manages merchant onboarding and the customer sees eligible payment
methods on the hosted invoice checkout.
